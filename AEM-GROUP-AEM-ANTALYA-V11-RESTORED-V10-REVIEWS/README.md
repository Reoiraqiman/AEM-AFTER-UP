# AEM GROUP — Vercel / Local SEO build

Production domain: https://aem-antalya.com/

## SEO implemented
- LocalBusiness + WebSite structured data on home pages
- Service + BreadcrumbList structured data on service pages
- Reciprocal hreflang for TR/EN/RU/AR service pages
- Self-referencing canonical URLs
- Localized title and meta descriptions
- Open Graph + Twitter metadata with absolute URLs
- XML sitemap with home, service and Turkish guide URLs
- robots.txt and Vercel security headers
- 404 page
- Turkish topical guide pages for long-tail search intent
- Mobile-first CTA and internal linking

## Important
SEO cannot guarantee a #1 position. Local visibility also depends on the verified Google Business Profile, relevance, distance, prominence, genuine reviews, photos, citations and links.

After deployment, submit https://aem-antalya.com/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console and inspect the homepage + priority service URLs.
