Air Conditioner Gas Refilling in Antalya: When Can It Be Needed?

Poor cooling does not always mean that refrigerant is low. Filters, airflow, the outdoor unit, electrical faults or a refrigerant leak can also affect performance. A technical check before refilling is therefore important.
