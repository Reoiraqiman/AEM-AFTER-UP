FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Service requests are accepted across Antalya. Availability is assessed according to the device and request.

Yes. AC gas filling requests are accepted and the device condition is assessed during the service process.

Do you repair and install air conditioners?

Yes. You can request AC fault diagnosis and repair, installation or removal.

Requests are accepted for refrigerators, washing machines, dishwashers and boilers.
